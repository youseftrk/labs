# Voice

How Yousef writes on this site. Read this before adding or rewriting copy. Skills behind it: `unslop`, `letter-landing`, `better-writing`.

## Who is talking

Grade 12 at Liwa in Abu Dhabi. AI for science and startups, same week. He writes research he can stand behind and ships products with a number attached. Applying to MBZUAI. He has read Taste Labs. He loves Marvel and Tony Stark most of all, and says Stark resembles him: build the rough version first, then do it properly. He likes Bourdain because context beats the perfect plate (Bourdain is off home since 2 Sep 2026, still in `content/home.ts`).

## Sound

- First person. Contractions. `I'm`, `don't`, `it's`.
- Short then long. Do not march in matching pairs (`X got cheap. Y did not.`).
- One concrete thing per paragraph. A class, a paper, a number, a tool.
- Explain the term where you use it. On a research page every line has to stand on its own: if you write VAE, Perturb-seq, attractor, or neuromorphic, the same sentence says what it means in plain words. A reader who does not know the field should finish the page understanding it. No glossary, no assumed background, no jargon left hanging.
- High agency is shown, not announced. "I enter the thing I'm underqualified for, then close the gap before the deadline" plus the result that proves it. Never "passionate", never "driven".
- Opinions. If you do not care, cut the sentence.
- Mess is allowed. A leftover thought. A joke that is dry, not cute.
- Sentence case titles.

## Do not

- Parallel slogans and manifesto cadence
- `not just X, but Y`
- `table stakes`, `tractable`, `the floor`, `live brief`, `seam shows up` unless he already said them once and you are quoting yourself
- Em dashes, parenthetical asides stacked in a row, colon-as-drumroll
- Explaining the lab to the reader (`kept here so the catalog reaches`)
- Pretending he works at Taste Labs or FutureHouse
- Lettermarks. No initial in a squircle as a stand-in for a photo.

## Keep

- Honest dates
- Credit Taste Labs, Jakub, Lucas Jin when the page is their work. Do not name Arlan.
- Lab as a subpage you send, not a brand
- Matter 300
- Motion with a job, under 300ms except one slow reveal

## Test

Read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it in an interview, rewrite it.
